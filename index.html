Our Vision & Philosophy

Turning potential into opportunity — one child at a time.

What started as a roadside encounter with a few sharp kids in Sector 55, Gurugram, soon became a life-skills programme for children of housing society maids, domestic help, and security guards. As word spread through Ghata Village, more and more children turned up to meet “Joshi Ma'am”. The class grew from a small guest room, to a parking lot, to a rented room, to a neighbourhood park.

Today, Adesh Joshi Memorial Trust runs “Just for Myself” — an organisation dedicated to the educational empowerment of children from economically weaker sections of society. We groom approximately 100 first-generation learners: putting them in schools, tutoring them, instilling confidence through cultural programmes, field trips, summer camps, and skill-building — while also providing medical support whenever required.

  • Child Education

    Putting children in schools, tutoring, and running skill programmes for first-generation learners from economically weaker backgrounds, without distinction of caste, colour, or creed.

  • Community Support

    Distribution of free food, rations, bags, stationery, and essential supplies to the poor and needy. Establishing study centres for awareness and training.

  • Care & Welfare

    Medical relief for children of migrant labourers. Providing equipment like tricycles, crutches, and learning aids to physically impaired children. Safety and hygiene support for deprived women.
